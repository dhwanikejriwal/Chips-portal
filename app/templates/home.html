Process

How it works

Five steps from your initial application to receiving your official UIDAI Aadhaar Operator ID.

1
Register & verify
Fill the form, verify mobile OTP, upload your documents.
2
DC selection
District Collector reviews your application and selects candidates.
3
LMS training
Complete all mandatory UIDAI e-learning modules online.
4
NSEIT exam
Book and pass the 100-question certification exam.
5
Activation
Upload final docs and receive your UIDAI Operator ID.

Who can apply

Eligibility at a glance

Check these requirements before starting your application.

Educational qualification

  • 12th pass or 3-year diploma (general)
  • 10th pass for ASHA or Anganwadi workers
  • Original certificate required

Personal eligibility

  • Minimum age 18 years
  • Valid Aadhaar card mandatory
  • Not previously debarred by UIDAI

District & vacancy

  • Apply only in your home district
  • Vacancy must exist in district
  • One application per mobile number

Documents to upload

  • Aadhaar card scan or photo
  • PAN card and bank passbook
  • Qualification certificate and photograph
