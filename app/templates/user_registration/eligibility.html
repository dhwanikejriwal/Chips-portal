Eligibility Criteria

Before applying, make sure you meet all requirements. Incomplete or ineligible applications will be rejected at the verification stage.

Personal Eligibility
  • Age 18 years or above
  • Valid Aadhaar card
  • Not previously disqualified by UIDAI
Educational Qualification
  • 12th pass or 3-year diploma
  • 10th pass for ASHA / Anganwadi
Documents Needed
  • Aadhaar card
  • PAN card
  • Passbook / bank details
  • Qualification certificate
Process Overview

5 steps from registration to activation as a certified operator.

1
Register: Provide your details, upload documents, and verify your mobile number and E-mail via OTP.
2
Verification: Authorities will review the application. Eligible candidates are selected and notified.
3
LMS Training: Apply for LMS access. Credentials are issued by CHIPS via E-mail. Complete all e-learning modules.
4
NSEIT Exam: Request NSEIT exam slot. Take the 100-question certification exam and download your certificate.
5
Activation: Upload all activation documents. After authorities approval, UIDAI issues your operator ID.
